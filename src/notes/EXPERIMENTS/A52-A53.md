## 目标
提升 fulfill 分数（当前 1.078 → 目标 1.2+）

## 改动

### A52: iterations 优化
- `max_iterations`: 10 → 100 → 200

**结果**:
| iterations | fulfill score |
|------------|--------------|
| 10 | 1.078 (baseline) |
| 100 | 1.114 ✅ |
| 200 | 1.087 ❌ |

**结论**: iterations=100 最优

### A53: neighbor_size 优化
- `neighbor_size`: 5 → 8 → 10

**结果**:
| neighbor_size | fulfill score |
|---------------|--------------|
| 5 | 1.093 |
| 8 | 1.117 ✅ |
| 10 | 1.109 |

**结论**: neighbor_size=8 最优

int max_iterations = 100;  // A52: optimal
